1. Overview
This system helps track products from farmers to consumers, ensuring transparency and traceability.
Actors: Farmer, Logistics, Retailer, Consumer

2. Business Flow
1. Farmer creates product batch
2. Logistics updates transportation data
3. Retailer confirms product
4. Consumer views product journey

Farmer Screen
Input product name, harvest date, and location to create a batch.
